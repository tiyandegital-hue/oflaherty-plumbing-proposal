Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This is a concept demonstration website built by Tiyan Digital.

The short version

This demo site collects nothing. There is no server, no database, no analytics, no cookies, and no tracking. The contact form is simulated — anything you type stays in your own browser and is never sent anywhere.

What a real version of this site would collect

If this concept were turned into a live business website, the service-request form would collect your name, phone number, and job details — only what is needed to call you back about your request. That is the entire list. We would never ask for anything else on that form.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may log that a font file was requested; we set no cookies and run no analytics of our own. That is the full list of third parties on this page.

Your rights

Since we collect nothing, there is nothing to correct or delete. On a live version, you could email oflahertyph@sbcglobal.net at any time to ask what was stored about you, and it would be deleted on request.
